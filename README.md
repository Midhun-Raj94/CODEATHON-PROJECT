 Business Sales Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

 Project Overview

This project is an interactive "Business Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI".

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance by analyzing key sales metrics, product performance, sales trends, and regional distribution.

The objective of this project is to transform business transaction data into meaningful KPIs, visual insights, and actionable recommendations** that can support data-driven decision-making.


 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer important business questions:

 How is the business performing overall? What are the key sales and business KPIs?
 Which products/categories contribute most to business performance?
 How does sales performance change over time?
 Which regions generate the highest sales?
What business actions can be taken based on the observed trends?

 Key Insights
Based on the Power BI dashboard structure and the business objectives, these are the **5 key insights and recommendations**:

Key Insights & Recommendations

1. Profitability is the primary performance indicator

   * The dashboard tracks total profit and yearly profit trends, allowing management to identify periods of stronger and weaker profitability.
   * Recommendation:Investigate low-profit years and focus on improving pricing, cost control, and product-level margins.

2. Sales performance varies significantly by region

   * Regional sales and order distribution show that some regions contribute more strongly to overall business performance.
   * **Recommendation:** Prioritize high-performing regions while developing targeted promotions and sales strategies for underperforming regions.

3.A small number of products drive a significant share of demand

   * The "Top 5 Trending Products" visualization highlights products with the highest order quantities.
   * Recommendation: Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products and use their popularity for cross-selling and promotional campaigns.

4. Sales volume and profitability should be analyzed together

   * High sales or high order quantity does not necessarily mean high profit. The dashboard combines sales, units sold, transactions, and profit to support this comparison.
   * Recommendation: Identify products/regions with **high sales but low profit** and optimize their pricing, discounts, or operating costs.

5. Use historical trends for better business planning

   * The yearly profit trend provides a basis for identifying growth patterns and periods of declining performance.
   * Recommendation: Use historical sales and profit patterns to forecast demand, plan inventory, allocate marketing budgets, and set realistic future targets.
